What powers the signal

Loading methodology…

The ensemble combines Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and a nearest-neighbour regime model. A strong-looking probability is not treated as high conviction unless the models agree and recent time-ordered holdout performance supports it.

How to use this page

Probability Up asks whether gold is more likely to finish above today’s reference price after the selected horizon. 20–80% range is a probabilistic historical band, not a price target. Backtest edge compares the ensemble with a naive majority-direction baseline.
