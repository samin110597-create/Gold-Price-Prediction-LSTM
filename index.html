How to read this

Probability Up estimates whether price will finish above today’s level after the selected horizon. Forecast range is a historical 20th–80th percentile band, adjusted only slightly by the model. Backtest accuracy is shown beside a naive baseline so a weak model is visible instead of hidden.

This is an educational research tool, not a promise of future prices. Gold can move sharply on macro news, rates, currencies and geopolitical events that a price-only model cannot know in advance.
